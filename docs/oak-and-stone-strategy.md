**Plumbing/Electrical Modifier:**
- Yes → Add 12–18% to the base range
- Probably → Add 8% to upper end of range only
- No → No adjustment

**Timeline Modifier (urgency display only — no price change):**
- As Soon As Possible → Display: "We have limited project slots available — recommend scheduling a consultation within 7 days."
- Within 1–3 Months → Display: "Currently booking projects 4–6 weeks out."
- 3–6 Months → Standard display.
- Just Exploring → Display: "No rush — most homeowners plan 3–6 months ahead."

### SECTION 15 — FAQ

**Headline:**
> Questions We Hear Every Week

---

**How accurate is the online estimator?**
> Our estimator is based on over 200 completed projects in the Raleigh area and gives you a realistic range — not a lowball to get you in the door. Most final quotes land within 10–15% of the estimator range. The detailed consultation tightens that number to the exact figure.

**How long does a remodel take?**
> Bathroom remodels typically run 4–8 weeks. Kitchen remodels take 7–12 weeks. Home additions run 12–20 weeks depending on size. We give you a week-by-week schedule before any contract is signed.

**Do you offer financing?**
> Yes. We partner with two financing providers who offer 12-month same-as-cash and longer-term payment plans. Many homeowners pay as little as $299/month on qualifying projects.

**Can I stay in my home during the remodel?**
> For kitchen and bathroom projects: usually yes, with some adjustments during peak demolition. For whole-home remodels or additions that affect major utilities: we'll give you an honest recommendation during the consultation.

**Do I need permits for my remodel?**
> For any structural, plumbing, or electrical work — yes. We handle all permit applications and inspections in Wake County as part of our fixed-price proposal. You don't pay extra, and you don't have to manage it yourself.

**What areas do you serve?**
> Our primary service area covers Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Wake Forest, Garner, Holly Springs, Morrisville, and Fuquay-Varina. Contact us for projects outside Wake County.

**What if my project goes over budget?**
> It doesn't — that's the point of a fixed-price proposal. Once you sign, that number is your number. If we uncover something unexpected inside a wall, we discuss it with you in writing before proceeding. No surprise invoices.
